## Real bugs found and fixed (not just polish)
- **Crash**: a library-search focus hack looped with Android re-granting focus, causing a StackOverflowError on launch or return.
- **Lasso button bar placed against a sliver**: `RectF.isEmpty` is true for a single point, so the selection rect reset at every
  corner. The bar sat on top of the selection and off-centre. **Graphite has the same bug** (not touched).
- **New-notebook dialog**: the keyboard never hid, because the focus manager was read outside the dialog's own window.
- **Wheel gesture reset** (above).
- **Toolbar centring**: a saved drop position (along = 0.38) silently pulled the toolbar left.
